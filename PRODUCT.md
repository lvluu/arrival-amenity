# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Resort staff (front office / F&B / housekeeping) who prepare the daily fruit order for arriving villas. They use a phone,
often standing, in daylight, between other tasks. Today they read the Opera "Enhanced Pre Arrival Report" PDF by hand:
villa -> room type -> bedrooms -> fruit.

## Product Purpose

Turn the arrival PDF into the fruit order in seconds, and show proof for every number so staff trust it without
re-reading the report. Success: one glance gives the total and the per-villa list; one tap shows where in the PDF a number came from.

## Brand Personality

Plain, quick, trustworthy. A tool, not a showcase. Calm confidence: the number, then the proof.

## Anti-references

- Long scrolling pages, desktop tables squeezed onto a phone.
- SaaS dashboard kits: hero metrics, card grids, gradients.
- Anything that hides the source: numbers without a way to check them.

## Design Principles

1. One screen, no hunting: the total and the villa list fit a phone screen; detail opens in place.
2. Every number has its proof one tap away (red box on the source PDF).
3. Assumed rules are editable and visible: settings say what they change.
4. Guest data stays on the device; the app says so.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Tap targets >= 44px, high contrast for daylight use, keyboard and screen reader friendly, reduced motion respected.
